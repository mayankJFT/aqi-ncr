    Why this ledger exists

    The record is the pressure

    Everyone knows Delhi's air in November. This ledger is about the other ten months — the “clean” monsoon mornings when the air is still at several times the safe limit, and nobody calls it an emergency.

    So a machine reads the government's own sensors every morning at seven, holds them against the standard the WHO says a human body can bear, and writes it down. Publicly. Permanently. Every single day.

    Opinions can be dismissed. A daily record cannot.

    Method

    Open data · open code

    Source: Central Pollution Control Board real-time monitoring, via the Open Government Data Platform. Readings are pulled daily at 07:00 IST, station values averaged per city, sensor artefacts (≤0 or >1500 µg/m³) discarded, then benchmarked against WHO 2021 24-hour air quality guidelines. Every report is kept, day after day. Adapted from the open-source nashik-air-watch project. Nothing to trust; everything to verify.
