    Complaint ledger

    21-day response deadlines · tracked publicly
    No complaints documented yet.

    On every breach day, a formal complaint draft citing CPCB's own readings is generated and committed to the public repository — addressed to the responsible authorities (CAQM, CPCB, DPCC, UPPCB, HSPCB), with suggested delivery channels inside each draft. Drafts are never sent automatically: review one, file it via CPGRAMS or the relevant board, and the 21-day response clock starts from your filing date.

    Method

    open data · open code

    Source: Central Pollution Control Board real-time monitoring, via the Open Government Data Platform. Readings are pulled daily at 07:00 IST, station values averaged per city, sensor artefacts (≤0 or >1500 µg/m³) discarded, then benchmarked against WHO 2021 24-hour air quality guidelines. Every reading, chart and complaint draft in this ledger is generated and committed automatically, every morning, to github.com/mayankJFT/aqi-ncr — open data in, open record out.
