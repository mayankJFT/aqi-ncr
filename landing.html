How it works

every morning · 07:00 IST
01

Read

The government's own CPCB monitoring stations are read through the open data platform — every station, every pollutant, for five NCR cities.

02

Benchmark

City averages are held against the WHO 2021 24-hour guidelines — the standard a human body can bear, not a relaxed local norm.

03

Record

The verdict is committed to a public repository — charts, reports, and on breach days a drafted complaint with its 21-day response clock tracked in the open.
