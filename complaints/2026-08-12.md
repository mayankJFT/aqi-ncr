### Air Quality Complaint for Delhi NCR Region as of 2026-08-12

As per the real-time data available from the Central Pollution Control Board's (CPCB) stations via data.gov.in, the air quality in various cities of the Delhi NCR region has exceeded the World Health Organization (WHO) 2021 guidelines. Specifically, the readings for Particulate Matter (PM) 2.5 and PM 10 are as follows:
- Delhi: PM2.5 at 64.6 ug/m3 (4.3 times the WHO limit) and PM10 at 77.7 ug/m3 (1.7 times the WHO limit)
- Noida: PM2.5 at 66.3 ug/m3 (4.4 times the WHO limit) and PM10 at 77.5 ug/m3 (1.7 times the WHO limit)
- Ghaziabad: PM2.5 at 45.0 ug/m3 (3.0 times the WHO limit) and PM10 at 64.0 ug/m3 (1.4 times the WHO limit)
- Gurugram: PM2.5 at 168.0 ug/m3 (11.2 times the WHO limit) and PM10 at 126.0 ug/m3 (2.8 times the WHO limit)
- Faridabad: PM2.5 at 68.7 ug/m3 (4.6 times the WHO limit) and PM10 at 92.7 ug/m3 (2.1 times the WHO limit)

These readings indicate a significant breach of air quality standards, posing serious health risks to the residents of these areas. In accordance with the Air (Prevention and Control of Pollution) Act, 1981, it is imperative that immediate action is taken to mitigate these high levels of pollution.
